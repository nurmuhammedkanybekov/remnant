# Texture credits

The photo-scanned materials in this folder come from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com)
and are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain). The game paints its own
details on top of them at load time (panel seams, rivets, kick-plates, stains,
ceiling grid, crate frames) — see `src/fx/textures.ts`.

Files were resized and re-compressed for the web; nothing else was changed.
