# REMNANT — The campaign as one experience

Seventeen levels read as one journey, from the bed in the infirmary to the
cavity. For each: why it's there in the story, why it's there to play, what
you remember it by, and what has changed by the time you leave it, in you
and in the voice.

Everything in this document is **built** unless it is marked
**Proposed**. Built means in the level data and checked in the browser
(see `DIRECTION.md` → Status for what that checking does and doesn't
cover).

## The arc of the voice

| Part       | Levels | The voice is...                                                          | You learn...                                                     |
| ---------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Waking     | 1–3    | A helpful operator. It asks if you're alone, what you fear. It's right.  | Light, sound, hiding. That it knows things it shouldn't.         |
| Doubt      | 4–7    | Kind, and wanting things: leave the medkit, don't listen to the humming. | That someone else hums its tune, and someone else needs you.     |
| The lie    | 8–10   | It sends you into a room full of them, to see if you'll go.              | That it was testing you all along. Under the stars it says so.   |
| The valley | 11–14  | Back on the radio, everywhere. It asks where you're going.               | That it learned to speak from a transmitter, and you can cut it. |
| Going in   | 15–17  | Closer, quieter, pleading. Then a choir.                                 | Who taught Kessler the tune. What it kept of you.                |

## Level by level

### 1 Infirmary (Sublevel 10)

- **Story**: you wake. Mara kept you sedated with the radio unplugged; that's why you're still you.
- **Play**: movement, the torch, the first note, one Husk seen from safety.
- **Identity**: a hospital ward in a mine. Kessler's bed with straps.
- **Main interaction**: the intercom; the first conversation with the Operator.
- **Moment**: four notes hummed through the walls before you know what they are.
- **Optional**: Ward 1, chairs in a circle round a radio that's still on.
- **Connects to**: Kessler's tune (the whole campaign); Mara's rule (the questions).
- **Changes**: you have a voice in your ear, and a rule from Mara not to answer it.

### 2 Maintenance Wing (Sublevel 9)

- **Story**: the crew's radio came on by itself and claimed to be the surface.
- **Play**: stealth against Husks that hunt by sound.
- **Identity**: loops of service tunnel; a break-room radio with a chair pulled up to it.
- **Main interaction**: it asks "Are you alone?". Later it tells you to put your light out, and it's right.
- **Moment**: standing in the dark while something walks past on the other side of the wall.
- **Optional**: the 1988 vent, boarded up, three stools in front of it.
- **Connects to**: the vents (it reminds you you're alone); the end of both parts.
- **Changes**: you either trusted it once and it was right, or you didn't.

### 3 Cold Storage (Sublevel 8)

- **Story**: Hendricks and the samples; the Brute was three of the crew.
- **Play**: the first keycard door; the first Brute.
- **Identity**: freezers, breath in the air, three pairs of boots in a row.
- **Main interaction**: it asks what you're afraid of.
- **Moment**: "НЕ ГОВОРИ ЕМУ СВОЁ ИМЯ" painted over a radio that is on.
- **Optional**: Hendricks' GLASS crates and his detonator wire.
- **Connects to**: the containment blackout (if you said the dark, it lasts twice as long).
- **Changes**: it knows your fear, or knows you wouldn't say.
- **Proposed**: no authored moment yet. A freezer door that closes behind you when you take the keycard, and the voice offering to open it for you: an instruction you can refuse, and a debt if you don't.

### 4 Pumping Station (Sublevel 7)

- **Story**: Arkadin stopped the pumps and stopped talking; it learns from voices.
- **Play**: wading is loud; the first Listener, which is blind.
- **Identity**: flooded halls, the soundproofed room.
- **Main interaction**: it asks you to leave a medkit for Ruslan. No prompt: take it or don't.
- **Moment**: Kessler's tune, hummed by someone through the walls; follow it.
- **Optional**: Omurbek's note, the tune written down.
- **Connects to**: Ruslan at the power plant and at both ends; the trail to the photograph.
- **Changes**: someone you haven't met now depends on what you did.

### 5 Containment Labs (Sublevel 6)

- **Story**: R-7, Arkadin's daughter's drawing, Project GLASS.
- **Play**: the Watcher. Light is a weapon and a risk.
- **Identity**: the original 1980s laboratory, broken glass.
- **Main interaction**: a keycard, in the dark.
- **Moment**: the power dies in a wave that reaches you last; your torch fails; breathing behind you.
- **Optional**: the blood cabinet, one vial dark.
- **Connects to**: your answer in cold storage.
- **Changes**: you know the dark can be taken from you, and maybe that you handed it over.

### 6 Ventilation (Sublevel 5)

- **Story**: "THE VOICE IS NOT THE SUPERVISOR." The first crack.
- **Play**: tight ducts, Crawlers, silent takedowns, the rivet gun.
- **Identity**: a maze you count your way through.
- **Main interaction**: the Mimic speaks with the Operator's voice on an intercom. And the real voice asks you not to go towards the humming.
- **Moment**: realising there are two voices, and you can't tell them apart.
- **Optional**: Asanov's note: Kessler didn't learn the tune, he remembered it. The 1991 camp, 212 days.
- **Connects to**: the Hive ("What did he tell you, the man with the nail?").
- **Changes**: the first time it wants something for itself.

### 7 Power Plant (Sublevel 4)

- **Story**: the lift needs power; Arkadin: it wants to be carried.
- **Play**: three generators, each a dinner bell.
- **Identity**: turbine halls, Spitters.
- **Main interaction**: start a generator, then get away from it.
- **Moment**: Ruslan on the radio: standing, thanking whoever left the kit, or crawling to where it was.
- **Optional**: the 1991 engineer's crossed-out list.
- **Connects to**: the pumping station; the recount.
- **Changes**: what you did two levels ago has a face, or at least a voice.

### 8 Armory (Sublevel 3)

- **Story**: Mara: every time it talks to you it leaves a little of itself behind.
- **Play**: the shotgun, a big arena, Swarms.
- **Identity**: an arms store that was already overrun.
- **Main interaction**: it tells you to hide in the store. It's a lie.
- **Moment**: going in, and hearing them come; or not going, and it says "Good. You're learning. So am I."
- **Optional**: Mara's note is on the table in the room it sends you to.
- **Connects to**: every instruction after this one; the recount.
- **Changes**: you know it lies. Everything it says afterwards is in doubt.

### 9 The Hive (Sublevel 2)

- **Story**: the Operator stops pretending. Mara, absorbed, calling you in.
- **Play**: the boss.
- **Identity**: soft, warm walls.
- **Main interaction**: the fight.
- **Moment**: "We are in every room." The tune from inside the walls.
- **Optional**: Mara's photograph of the valley.
- **Connects to**: the duct in the vents (it remembers); Part Two (the valley).
- **Changes**: the voice is a "we".

### 10 The Portal (Surface)

- **Story**: Arkadin's door opens only for the unchanged. Seal or leave.
- **Play**: the last climb, then out under the sky.
- **Identity**: stars. Silence. Peaks.
- **Main interaction**: the choice.
- **Moment**: under the stars, it tells you what it kept of you: four moments, in order, and then "walk us down". Then three knocks on the inside of the door.
- **Optional**: the roster: forty-one names, forty crossed out.
- **Connects to**: everything in Part One.
- **Changes**: you know it was paying attention the whole time.

### 11 Clinic (Ak-Suu)

- **Story**: three weeks later; the voice is back.
- **Play**: a small, quiet level; Husks and a Listener.
- **Identity**: a village clinic, the waiting-room radio.
- **Main interaction**: it asks where you're going.
- **Moment**: everything goes silent, then the waiting-room radio switches itself on.
- **Optional**: the duty log.
- **Connects to**: the dam (if you told it, they're waiting).
- **Changes**: it's no longer in a mountain; it's in every radio in the valley.

### 12 The Village (Ak-Suu)

- **Story**: every door open, every house warm.
- **Play**: the Howler: if it sees you, everything hears.
- **Identity**: snow, timber houses, lamps on wires.
- **Main interaction**: the garage key. And it tells you not to move.
- **Moment**: the villagers in the snow with their backs to you, gone in your light.
- **Optional**: Aibek's note: granny hums it over the washing.
- **Connects to**: the Source (what was behind you).
- **Changes**: you've stood still for it, or you haven't.

### 13 Hydro Dam (Kara-Suu)

- **Story**: hundreds in the reservoir, waiting.
- **Play**: three generators for the spillway.
- **Identity**: the open reservoir under the sky.
- **Main interaction**: generators again.
- **Moment**: three figures standing in the water under the shore lamp.
- **Optional**: the gate log.
- **Connects to**: the clinic's question.
- **Changes**: little. **This is the weakest level structurally**: a second generator level.
- **Proposed**: replace the generators with the spillway's sirens. Each gate you open sounds a siren that every creature hears; you choose the order, and the voice tells you which is safest (it's honest, here, if you've obeyed it more than you defied it, and lies otherwise).

### 14 Observatory (Kosh-Tash)

- **Story**: it was never a control room; it learned to speak from a transmitter.
- **Play**: cut three relay breakers; it changes as each goes.
- **Identity**: the ridge, the dish, cold.
- **Main interaction**: silencing it.
- **Moment**: the channel goes dead; then the radio on your belt hums the tune, switched off.
- **Optional**: the cook's night shift.
- **Connects to**: Kessler's trail; the Source.
- **Changes**: you've taken its voice. It found another way.

### 15 Rail Tunnel (Drainage Line)

- **Story**: Sadykova found her son and stays with him. The 1987 photograph.
- **Play**: four kilometres of dark, alarms.
- **Identity**: rails, water, one way in.
- **Main interaction**: getting through.
- **Moment**: footsteps keeping pace behind you on the rails, then your torch dies.
- **Optional**: the survey photograph: Kessler in 1987, not a day younger.
- **Connects to**: the Source (Nur names where the tune came from).
- **Changes**: you know who taught Kessler.
- **Proposed**: navigation by sound. A long blackout stretch where the only guide is the hum coming down the tunnel from the cavity, and the voice offering to guide you instead.

### 16 Laboratory Zero (Beneath Ten)

- **Story**: Arkadin's first and last logs; he asked to be added.
- **Play**: everything at once.
- **Identity**: white tile, drains, the drill site.
- **Main interaction**: power for the cavity lift.
- **Moment**: the blackout at the drill site, someone pale by the water.
- **Optional**: Arkadin's second log: the first time it answered.
- **Connects to**: the Source.
- **Changes**: you're under the bed you woke up on.
- **Proposed**: fighting is possible but makes it worse: every creature killed here joins the Choir's voices at the Source (one more voice in the last fight).

### 17 The Source (The Cavity)

- **Story**: a choir; Mara's last note: it isn't angry, it's lonely.
- **Play**: the Choir.
- **Identity**: bare rock.
- **Main interaction**: the fight, then the choice: walk out or bring it down.
- **Moment**: when it's dead, what's left of it tells you what it kept of you. Then your ending's last line, from where you stood with it.
- **Optional**: Mara's last note.
- **Connects to**: the whole run.
- **Changes**: it's over, or it isn't.

## Structure, honestly

Shapes, after this pass (counted from the level data): walk 5, keycard 5,
keycard + relay 1 (the observatory), generators 2 (power plant, dam),
generators + keycard 1 (Laboratory Zero), boss 1 (the Hive), finale 2 (the
portal; the Source, with its boss).
The bids give five levels a decision that isn't the objective, but the
objectives themselves are still mostly "find the card". The three
**Proposed** redesigns above (the dam's sirens, the tunnel by sound,
Laboratory Zero's cost of fighting) are the next structural work, in that
order: the dam is the most repetitive, the tunnel the most promising.
